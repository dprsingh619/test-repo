Good to know

Travel lighter

The small details that make
the big moments smoother.

↗

Move in one direction

Seminyak → Ubud → Canggu keeps transfer days short and the good energy high.

◷

Chase cooler hours

Visit temples early or late, and save the middle of the day for shade, lunch or a pool.

✺

Pack a sarong

Temples require covered shoulders and knees. A light sarong solves it beautifully.

≈

Book the surf lesson

Choose an instructor-led session unless you already feel confident in the breaks.
